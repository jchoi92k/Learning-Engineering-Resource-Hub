Achievement and opportunity gaps were exacerbated by the pandemic, and students who were already furthest behind have struggled the most to recover. In schools across the country, instructional incoherence, particularly across core classrooms, intervention, and other academic supports, undermines learning acceleration and widens opportunity gaps. Coherence by Design: Building Instructional Systems That Help Students Catch Up makes the case that districts can transform students’ experiences and outcomes by aligning instruction, content, and goals across all learning settings.
