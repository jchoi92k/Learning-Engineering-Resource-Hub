Evidence review published at Evidence for ESSA (Johns Hopkins University). Carnegie Learning's Lenses on Literature (formerly Literacy Design Collaborative) earned a "Promising" (Tier 3) ESSA evidence rating. Instructional framework emphasizing daily, standards-driven reading, writing, and discourse organized around disciplinary literacy with complex texts. Primary study: 18,203 students across Kentucky and Pennsylvania (2012–2013). Effect size: +0.05 average; Kentucky: +0.06 (statistically significant); Pennsylvania: +0.03 (non-significant). Includes scaffolds for multilingual learners, embedded assessments using Stanford-aligned rubrics, and professional development with coaching and data analysis.

JUMP Math is a heavily scaffolded direct-instruction program covering all K-8 math strands, including computation and problem solving. Described as guided discovery, it gives teachers detailed, explicit guides for leading students to target concepts. Concepts are broken into ever smaller steps until mastered, then rebuilt step by step to curriculum level, with plenty of practice at each step and continuous assessment before moving on. The whole class works on the same concept while individuals go at their own pace with easier or harder work or extra practice. Models such as concrete materials, tables and diagrams help with abstract ideas and are withdrawn once students no longer need them. There are no textbooks: students use streamlined practice and assessment guides with only essential language, while lesson detail stays in the teacher's guide so teachers can pace information and avoid overloading working memory.

Parent Teacher Home Visits (PTHV), designed by parents and educators on shared-leadership organizing principles, has teachers make 30-40 minute home visits to listen and learn things that improve their teaching.
