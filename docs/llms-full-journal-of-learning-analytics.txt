
This research examines how students in grades 8–12 engage with mathematical functions creatively using function art, a STEAM-based method. The study involved 400 Philippine students creating digital artworks in GeoGebra. Through hierarchical clustering analysis, researchers identified three learner profiles: "Repetitivists" (quantity-focused), "Simplists" (limited use), and "Multifunctionists" (diversity-focused). The study addresses supporting academically vulnerable students by combining predictive machine learning models with theoretically-grounded intervention feedback.
