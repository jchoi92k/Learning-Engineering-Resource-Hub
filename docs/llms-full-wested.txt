
This study evaluated how NBA Math Hoops affects student math performance through a rigorous experimental design. The research found that "students in NBA Math Hoops classrooms scored an average of 0.19 standard deviations higher than peers in STEM enrichment classrooms" — roughly equivalent to advancing from the 50th to 58th percentile. The program demonstrated practical benefits for educators, requiring minimal training and costing under $20 per student while remaining straightforward to implement in out-of-school settings. This framework helps educators integrate AI intentionally while preserving meaningful learning. This WestEd evaluation examined how legislative funding was distributed across all 23 CSU campuses and its initial effects on AANHPI students.
