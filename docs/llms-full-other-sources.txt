
The 2018 Duolingo Shared Task on Second Language Acquisition Modeling (SLAM) provided participating teams with transcripts from millions of exercises completed by thousands of Duolingo learners over their first 30 days of study, annotated at the token level for mistakes, with the task of predicting future learner errors. Three tracks covered learners of English, Spanish, and French, enabling research into personalized adaptive SLA modeling and language forgetting over time; 15 teams participated and the best systems improved upon a logistic regression baseline by 12%. ```

Add Health is a nationally representative longitudinal panel study tracking a cohort of U.S. adolescents from grades 7–12 (1994–95) through early midlife, with data collection across six waves extending to 2025 when participants were ages 39–51. The study combines survey data on social, economic, psychological, and physical well-being with rich contextual information on families, schools, neighborhoods, and peer and romantic relationships; later waves add biospecimens, physical measurements, and comprehensive life history data. The STEP (Skills Toward Employment and Productivity) Skills Measurement Program is the World Bank's first initiative to generate internationally comparable data on workforce skills in low- and middle-income countries.
