REL Appalachia (REL 2024-005) examined how northeastern Tennessee school districts implemented the Niswonger Foundation's Project On-Track literacy tutoring initiative for early elementary students. description_inferred: false
description_source: llm-summary
date_added: 2026-05-01
doi: null
license: null
tags: [early-childhood, k-12, literacy, meta-analysis]
```

REL Southeast (REL 2021-084) synthesized over 74,000 studies published between 1997 and 2017, ultimately identifying 132 evidence-based early literacy interventions across 109 high-quality experimental and quasi-experimental studies. The review found that domain-specific instruction reliably increases performance in that domain, and that combined phonological awareness and print knowledge instruction shows promise for improving decoding and early writing skills. Language-focused instruction was found to be more effective in small group or one-on-one settings.

REL Mid-Atlantic (REL 2025-009) tested Bayesian hierarchical modeling as a method to reduce the impact of random error on school accountability indicators in New Jersey, particularly for smaller student groups. description_source: llm-summary
date_added: 2026-05-01
doi: null
license: null
tags: [english-learners, higher-ed, longitudinal]
```

REL Pacific (REL 2026-006) evaluated a 2021-22 revision to the College of the Marshall Islands' ELA placement system that added a multiple-choice test and a writing component alongside high school GPA. Only 11% of first-time students placed into credit-bearing ELA courses under the new system compared to 22% previously; students in intensive pathways of credit-bearing courses showed the highest passing rates. The writing component did not predict any outcomes, leading the authors to recommend CMI consider removing it and weighting high school GPA more heavily.

REL Pacific (REL 2025-007) conducted a systematic review of supplemental literacy programs for K–3 students in the Northern Mariana Islands, where in 2019 only 24% of grade 3 students were reading at or above grade level. REL Northwest (REL 2025-011) examined how Alaska schools implement trauma-engaged practices using the state's Transforming Schools Framework, drawing on administrative data and surveys. REL West (REL 2021-129) examined principal retention across Arizona, Nevada, and Utah, finding that fewer than half of principals in each state remained at the same school over the four-year period from fall 2016 to fall 2020.
