This report examines state-level "free college" promise programs and finds they work best when "generous, easy to access, and paired with dedicated advising support." Although roughly one-third of first-year students attend college tuition-free, living expenses remain a heavy burden—low-income families spend about 38% of income on non-tuition costs after aid.
